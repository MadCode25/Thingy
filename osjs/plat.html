Welcome to OSjs

OSjs is a website from AmadOS made to
simulate (or probably just emulate) earliest operating systems made by AOS,
some of these are not known, and they also have a PC screen-like interface for that realistic OS vibe.


So, what do you want to try?

We have plenty of OSes here, D-OS CTX74, D-OS 1, 2, or the G-OS KK100.

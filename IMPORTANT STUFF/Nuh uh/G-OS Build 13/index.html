Get started with G-OS

Hi, welcome to G-OS, you can do anything, here are things you can do.

First, you can open any app, to close that specific app, click the icon where it came from.
Second, you can log out anytime.
Third, you can customize this OS, change the audio, or commands, and etc.
Tip: Press F11 (full screen) for a better interface, you can change back by pressing F11 again.
